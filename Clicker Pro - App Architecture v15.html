Part A

What & Who

What the app does, who uses it, and how the screens connect — read this part first.
A.0 — App Overview & Core Flows

How a Booking Moves Through the App

Everything in Clicker Pro revolves around one object: the booking (called an Event internally). A studio owner creates it, a team gets assigned to it, money moves against it, and an invoice gets generated from it — with a copy of both the booking and the payment quietly landing in Google Calendar and Google Sheets along the way.
Fig. 1 — The booking lifecycle, the spine of the entire app
CORE
The Five Things This App Does
  • 1. Books events — captures client, date, shift, venue, package, and team in one form, and warns the owner if there's a scheduling conflict.
  • 2. Manages a team — passcode-only joining, then proper job roles (Manager, HR, Editor, and more) with their own permissions.
  • 3. Tracks money — separates studio income from freelancer income, shows who owes what, and turns a booking into an invoice with one tap.
  • 4. Runs offline, backed up everywhere — built for patchy Bangladesh mobile networks; every screen works without internet, and every booking and payment also lands in Google Calendar and Google Sheets.
  • 5. Feels alive — full-screen pre-event alerts, premium motion throughout, and seasonal vibes for Eid, the World Cup, and other major occasions.
A.1 — Primary Roles & Permissions

Four Ways to Use the Marketplace

A Primary Role is chosen once, at signup, and never changes inside the app. It answers one question only: how does this person participate in the photography marketplace? It has nothing to do with their job title inside any one studio — that's a separate system, see A.2.
R-01 · Mobile
Freelancer
Has no studio. Gets booked by studio owners over the phone, and logs that booking themselves. Max 2 events/day, always. Tracks earnings across every studio they work for.
R-02 · Mobile
Owner
Runs a studio. Full booking form, client & payment tracking, no daily booking cap. Builds a team and assigns Team Roles to everyone who joins.
R-03 · Mobile
Both
Runs a studio AND freelances for other studios. Picks a mode each time they add a booking. Studio money and freelance money never mix.
R-04 · Web
Web Admin
Platform-level control panel, not part of the mobile app or any studio. User list, block/unblock, feature toggles, broadcast messages, version control.
⚑
Manager is no longer a Primary RoleEarlier versions of this spec had Manager as a 4th mobile role alongside Freelancer/Owner/Both. That's been replaced — Manager is now one job title inside an Owner's Team Role system (A.2), exactly like HR or Editor. A person's Primary Role is still just Freelancer, Owner, or Both; their Team Role is a separate, Owner-assigned layer on top.

A.2 — Team Roles & Joining

Every Studio Builds Its Own Org Chart

Once someone joins an Owner's team, the Owner gives them a Team Role — a job title that exists only inside that one studio, with its own permission set. An Owner can use the built-in templates or invent entirely new roles. The same person can hold completely different Team Roles at two different studios — Team Roles never travel with the person, only Primary Roles do.
Fig. 2 — Passcode-only joining, then Owner assigns a Team Role
JOIN
Passcode-Only Team Joining
Changed
PurposeOne clean, secure way to join a team — replaces manual entry and Gmail-based invites entirely.
  • Manual team-member entry and Gmail/Google-account invites are both removed — passcode is the only path in
  • Owner generates a passcode from Team Management (rotatable, can be set to expire or be single-use)
  • The joining person — Freelancer, Owner, or Both — enters that passcode inside their own app to request access
  • This drops them into a Pending list visible only to the Owner — they are not yet an active team member and see no studio data
  • The Owner approves from Pending and, in the same action, assigns a Team Role — only after that are they active and able to see studio-specific data
  • The Owner can reject a pending request with no notification sent, or revoke an active member's access at any time

ROLES
Team Role Templates & Custom Roles
New
PurposeLets a studio's internal structure actually match how it's run, instead of forcing every helper into one generic "Manager" bucket.
  • Four built-in templates ship with sensible default permissions already filled in: Manager, HR, Editor, Office Boy
  • Picking a template pre-fills its permission toggles; the Owner can still adjust any individual toggle afterward
  • "+ Create Custom Role" lets the Owner name anything — Accountant, Drone Pilot, Second Shooter Lead — and build its permissions from a blank toggle sheet
  • Every Team Role, template or custom, is built from the same underlying permission set: view bookings, manage bookings, pay freelancers, see income/profit, post announcements, manage gear, add other team members
  • A studio can have any number of Team Roles active at once, and any number of people holding the same one
Default template permissions
Manager
Manage bookings, pay freelancers, post announcements. Income/profit hidden by default.
HR
View team roster, manage Team Role assignments and passcodes. No booking or finance access.
Editor
View bookings and delivery checklists only — for marking edited/delivered status. No finance access.
Office Boy
View today's schedule only. No edit rights anywhere.
◆
This is not the Primary Role systemA Freelancer who joins a studio as its "Editor" is still a Freelancer everywhere else in the app — their own bookings, their own earnings dashboard, their FL Suite, all unaffected. Team Role only governs what they can see and do inside that one Owner's studio data.

B.2 — Booking Engine

Create, List, View, Track a Booking

This is the busiest part of the app. A booking is created here, lives in a list, opens into a detail screen, moves through a status workflow, and feeds an invoice — while quietly mirroring itself into Google Calendar and Google Sheets.
MOD-07
Booking Form — Owner / Both
CoreP2Confirmed
PurposeThe single form that creates every studio booking — fast enough to fill out one-handed between two phone calls.
  • A Save button stays pinned at the top of the screen at all times, even while scrolling
  • Only four fields are required: Client Name, Client Phone, Date, Shift — everything else is optional
  • An Owner can save any number of bookings per day with no toggle required — there has never been an Owner-side daily cap (see A.1)
  • In the Both role, a mode picker sits at the very top of the form — choosing between this Owner form and the short Freelancer form (FL-12)
  • Event types offered: Wedding, Holud, Reception, Corporate, Birthday, Engagement, Aqiqah, Portrait, Other
  • Team section: a gold Chief Photographer toggle (reveals a name field), then separate "+ Photographer" and "+ Cinematographer" groups, each opening a searchable multi-select sheet of active team members
  • Payment fields: Total + Advance (auto-filled from the package) + Method (bKash/Bank/Cash) + a hide-from-team eye toggle for sensitive amounts
  • There is no invoice section inside this form — saving shows a confirmation with an "Invoice →" button that opens the invoice separately (see MOD-14)
  • If another booking already exists for the same date and shift, the form shakes and shows a red conflict warning instead of silently overwriting it
  • Saving a confirmed booking also queues a Google Calendar event and a Google Sheets row in the background (see B.6) — this never blocks or slows down the Save action itself

B.5 — Freelancer Suite

Built for the Person Working for Many Studios

A freelance photographer doesn't have a studio of their own, but they might work for ten different ones in a month. This suite exists entirely to make that life easier — tracking pay across studios, blocking out dates, and recording bookings without any of the studio-side complexity.
FL-12
Freelancer Personal Booking
FLP2Final
PurposeThe short form a freelancer uses to log a booking themselves, the moment a studio calls to book them — this is the entire point of the Freelancer role existing as its own thing.
  • A top tab toggle switches between "Freelancer" and a specific company name; terracotta highlights the active tab
  • Fields: Company Name, Date, Shift (Day/Night toggle), an Outdoor switch that expands to reveal Location + Reporting Time fields, Event Type chips, and a free-text Notes field
  • Deliberately missing, by design: no Booking Status field, no Due Payment Request, and no Invoice — a freelancer logging a job isn't billing anyone
  • A conflict banner warns if the freelancer is already booked by an Owner on that same date
  • In the Both role, the Add Booking flow opens a mode picker first: Freelancer mode opens this short form, Owner mode opens the full MOD-07 form with no daily cap
⚑
Booking limit — exact ruleAs a Freelancer, this form hard-blocks at 2 bookings per day (one Day shift, one Night shift) by default. If that Freelancer's own Distribution Mode is switched on, the 2-per-day cap lifts and they may log multiple overlapping bookings in a day. An Owner never had a daily cap to begin with, with or without Distribution Mode — for an Owner, Distribution Mode instead controls whether bookings made by other people are allowed to collide on that Owner's calendar.

B.6 — Google Sync

Every Booking Also Lives in Google

Clicker Pro is the source of truth, but nothing here should only exist in one place. Every confirmed booking and every recorded payment is mirrored out to the Owner's own Google account automatically — a safety net the Owner already trusts, with zero extra effort.
MOD-61
Google Calendar Sync
SystemP2Updated
PurposePuts every booking on the calendar app an Owner already checks all day, without them doing anything extra.
  • The moment a booking is saved as Confirmed or later, a matching event is created on the Owner's connected Google Calendar — title, date, shift time block, venue, and a link back into the app
  • Editing the booking inside the app updates the same Calendar event rather than creating a duplicate
  • Cancelling the booking removes the Calendar event
  • This is a one-way mirror: Clicker Pro → Google Calendar. Edits made directly inside Google Calendar do not write back into the app, to avoid silent data conflicts
  • Connection is per-Owner, set up once from Profile & Settings (MOD-03); if disconnected, bookings simply stop syncing until reconnected — nothing already synced is deleted
SHEETS
Google Sheets Sync — Bookings & Payments
FinanceP2New
PurposeGives every Owner an automatic, always-current backup ledger in a format they can open, filter, and hand to an accountant without needing the app at all.
  • On first connection, Clicker Pro creates one Google Sheet in the Owner's own Drive with two tabs: Bookings and Payments
  • Bookings tab — one row per booking: date, client, phone, shift, package, team, total, advance, due, status. A new row is appended the moment a booking is saved; an edited booking updates its existing row rather than duplicating it
  • Payments tab — one row per payment: date, client, booking reference, amount, method, recorded by. Appended the instant a payment is recorded anywhere in the app
  • Like Calendar sync, this is one-way (app → Sheet) and per-Owner; a Manager paying a freelancer, an Owner recording an advance, and an Editor marking a booking delivered all write through the same pipeline
  • Sync is queued and retried in the background — a spotty connection delays the Sheet update, it never blocks the in-app action or loses the row

SystemP2
PurposeMakes the app fully usable with no internet connection — the single most important system module for this market.

C.3 — Motion & Luxury Interactions

Animation as a Quality Signal

A studio owner judges software the way they judge a camera — by how it feels in the hand. Every motion in Clicker Pro is deliberate: confirms an action, draws attention to what matters, or makes a wait feel shorter. Nothing animates just to animate.
MOTION-01
Signature Moments
UXP2New
PurposeA small set of high-craft animations reserved for genuinely significant moments, so they keep their impact instead of becoming wallpaper.
  • Booking confirmed — a soft shutter-iris closes and reopens around the confirmation card, then a brief confetti burst in the active theme's accent color
  • Payment received — a coin glides from the amount field into the Collection total with a satisfying weighted drop, the total ticking upward as it lands
  • Invoice sent — the invoice card lifts slightly, tilts on a 3D axis as if being handed over, and slides off-screen toward the share sheet
  • Badge earned (Freelancer) — a gold-rimmed medallion rotates into view with a light bloom, then settles with a gentle bounce
  • Theme switch — a radial wipe from the toggle's position repaints the entire screen in the new theme in under 400ms, rather than a flat instant swap
MOTION-02
Ambient & Loading Motion
UXP2New
PurposeMakes ordinary waiting and scrolling feel premium instead of just functional.
  • Dashboard sections fade and lift into place in a staggered sequence on load (each block roughly 60ms behind the previous), instead of popping in all at once
  • An aperture-blade loading spinner (rotating shutter leaves) replaces a generic spinner anywhere content is fetching
  • Shimmer skeleton placeholders use a soft diagonal sweep in the active theme's accent tone, not a generic grey
  • Pull-to-refresh on the Booking List triggers a small camera-shutter "click" animation at the top of the screen instead of a plain spinner
  • Card press states use a subtle scale-down (98%) with a soft shadow contraction, so every tap feels acknowledged
MOTION-03
Transition Grammar
UXP3New
PurposeConsistent rules for how screens enter and leave, so navigation feels coherent rather than ad-hoc.
  • Forward navigation (List → Details) slides in from the right with a slight scale-up of the destination, depth-of-field style
  • Bottom sheets (team picker, invoice preview, filter panel) spring up with a gentle overshoot-and-settle, never a hard stop
  • The Add Booking FAB expands into the booking form with a camera-shutter iris transition — the signature transition of the whole app
  • Modals dim the background with a soft blur rather than a flat black scrim, keeping context visible underneath
✎
Performance guardrailEvery animation in this catalogue is capped at 400ms and built to degrade gracefully on lower-end Android devices common in this market — motion should read as luxury, never as lag. See C.6 Production & Reliability for the performance budget this is measured against.

C.6 — Production & Reliability

Hardening for Real-World Use

A non-coder, AI-assisted build needs a safety net more than most apps do. This layer hardens Clicker Pro for real-world use without over-engineering for a scale the launch user base doesn't need yet.
PROD-01
Data Protection
SystemP1
PurposeMakes sure a mistake — a bad delete, a bad sync, a bad migration — is always recoverable.
  • Automatic daily database backups, retaining the most recent set
  • An additional weekly consolidated snapshot, timed to align with the 7-day delete grace period
  • Soft-delete everywhere: a deleted record is flagged with a deleted_at timestamp, not actually erased, and can be recovered within 7 days
  • Recovery tools to restore either a soft-deleted record or a full backup snapshot
  • Versioned database migrations — no destructive schema change happens without a backup first
PROD-02
Observability & Crash Detection
SystemP1
PurposeKnow about a problem before a user has to complain about it.
  • Crash reporting via Firebase Crashlytics — auto-captures where it happened, the full stack trace, device model and OS, and how many users were affected
  • Non-fatal bug capture for handled errors that aren't full crashes; structured error logging with severity levels
  • Audit logs covering sensitive actions — payments, deletions, permission changes, Team Role assignments, payouts
  • Sync health monitoring — a failed Google Calendar or Sheets sync is logged and retried, and surfaces in Settings if it can't resolve itself after several attempts
PROD-03
Recovery & Versioning
SystemP2
PurposeA clear, repeatable process for undoing a bad release rather than scrambling.
  • A rollback system to revert to the last known-good build, paired with a staging-before-production rule
  • Full version control via Git, with tagged releases so any past version can be restored

PROD-04
Security
SystemP1
PurposeStandard, non-negotiable protections so user data and sessions can't be casually compromised.
  • JWT + refresh token authentication, OTP verification, and secure password hashing — attached to MOD-01
  • HTTPS-only traffic, SQL-injection and XSS protection on the backend, input validation, and global exception handling
  • Passcodes are rate-limited against brute-force guessing and can be rotated or revoked instantly by the Owner
  • Google OAuth tokens for Calendar/Sheets sync are stored encrypted and scoped to the minimum permissions needed
PROD-05
Performance (As Data Grows)
SystemP2
PurposeKeeps the app fast as a studio accumulates years of bookings.
  • Pagination and lazy loading on the Booking List and other long lists
  • Database indexing on frequently queried fields — date, shift, owner_id, status
  • Background jobs / a queue for heavy tasks like PDF generation, reminders, exports, and Google sync

Deliberately deferred — revisit at large scaleRedis caching, Grafana + Prometheus monitoring, Docker orchestration, and a full multi-environment CI pipeline are out of scope for launch. They add cost and complexity the current user base doesn't need yet — bring them in once concurrent usage actually grows.
C.7 — Locked Rules

Non-Negotiable Decisions

These are the rules that have already been argued through and settled. Any future change request that contradicts one of these should be flagged as a deliberate change, not a quiet drift. Rules changed in this version are marked.
Freelancer booking limit · updated
Max 2 per day (one Day + one Night) by default. Switching on their own Distribution Mode lifts this cap for the Freelancer themself.
Owner booking limit · confirmed
No cap, ever, with or without Distribution Mode. For an Owner, Distribution Mode instead governs whether others' bookings may collide on their calendar.
Manager is not a Primary Role · changed
Manager, HR, Editor, Office Boy, and any custom title are all Team Roles, assigned by an Owner per studio. Primary Role is only Freelancer / Owner / Both.
Team joining is passcode-only · changed
Manual entry and Gmail/Google-account invites are removed. A passcode plus Owner approval plus a Team Role assignment is the only path onto a team.
Team Roles don't travel
A Team Role exists only inside the one studio that assigned it. The same person can be "Editor" at one studio and "Manager" at another.
Invoice timing
Never inside the booking form. Always generated from Event Details, after the booking is saved.
Invoice content
Package name only (no pricing breakdown) + team names and phones + company name and phone. Any empty field is hidden, not shown blank.
FL-12 has no status
A freelancer's self-recorded booking has no Status field, no Due Payment Request, and no Invoice.
Both role finance
Studio income and Freelancer income live in two completely separate tabs — never combined into one number.
Google sync is one-way · new
Clicker Pro → Google Calendar / Sheets only. Edits made directly in Google are never pulled back in, to avoid silent overwrite conflicts.
Language is English-only · changed
No Bangla interface, no language picker, no in-app toggle. MOD-48 Bilingual System is removed. The ৳ symbol stays as a currency mark.
No phone-frame wrapper
Always rendered as a full mobile screen in the build — never inside a decorative phone-frame border.
Theme system · changed
Deep Ocean is retired. Sunset Studio (default) and Sunrise Pulse are the only two themes — two distinct identities, never a recolor of one.
Display font is scoped · new
Mood Booster is used only for Dashboard titles and section headers. Every value, label, and body line stays in the regular supporting font.
Occasion skins never touch data · new
An occasion theme changes accent colors and a few decorative motifs only — never layout, font, or functional behavior — and reverts automatically when its date window ends.
Data safety
Daily + weekly backups. Soft-delete with recovery. Audit log on every sensitive action, including Team Role changes. Always rollback-ready.
